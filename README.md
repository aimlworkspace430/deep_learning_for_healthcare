# Deep Learning for Healthcare Applications

This repository contains the **class activities, practical implementations, and experiments** conducted as part of the course       **BM4121:Deep Learning for Healthcare Applications**.

## Course Syllabus

### Unit I – Learnability & Introduction to Deep Learning
- Probability Distributions
- Bayesian Statistics
- Bias-Variance
- Overfitting & Underfitting
- Hoeffding's Inequality
- VC Dimension
- Artificial Neurons & Perceptron
- MLP and Feedforward Neural Networks
- Activation & Loss Functions
- Backpropagation and Gradient Descent

### Unit II – Training Deep Neural Networks
- Weight Initialization
- GD, SGD, Momentum, RMSProp, Adam
- Batch Normalization
- Dropout
- L1/L2 Regularization
- Hyperparameter Tuning
- Learning Rate Scheduling
- Early Stopping
- Model Evaluation

### Unit III – Convolutional Neural Networks
- Computer Vision & Image Representation
- Convolution, Padding, Stride & Pooling
- CNN Architectures
- LeNet, AlexNet, VGGNet, GoogLeNet, ResNet
- Transfer Learning & Fine-Tuning
- Image Classification
- Introduction to Object Detection

### Unit IV – Sequential Deep Learning Models
- RNN
- Vanishing & Exploding Gradients
- LSTM and GRU
- Bidirectional RNN
- Sequence-to-Sequence Learning
- Attention Mechanism
- NLP, Speech & Time-Series Applications

### Unit V – Advanced Deep Learning
- Transformers & Self-Attention
- Vision Transformers (ViT)
- Autoencoders & VAEs
- GANs
- Explainable AI: Grad-CAM & SHAP
- Edge AI & TinyML
- Deep Learning for Healthcare
- Biomedical Imaging
- Ethical & Responsible AI


> **This repository will be updated throughout the course with new class activities and implementations.**
